# Appendix A10 — Annual Report reconciliation

*We cross-checked our figures against NovaCorp's published FY2025 Annual Report to confirm we were describing the same workforce. Headcounts matched exactly. The attrition metric did not match its label.*

---

## 1. What matched

| Check | Annual Report | Our data | Match |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active employees | 12,003 | 12,003 | ✅ exact |
| Departures in period | 1,400 | 1,400 | ✅ exact |
| Retail Banking headcount | 2,974 | 2,974 | ✅ exact |
| Technology | 2,717 | 2,717 | ✅ exact |
| Risk & Compliance | 1,783 | 1,783 | ✅ exact |
| Insurance | 1,597 | 1,597 | ✅ exact |
| Wealth Management | 1,387 | 1,387 | ✅ exact |
| Corporate Operations | 1,334 | 1,334 | ✅ exact |
| Executive Leadership | 211 | 211 | ✅ exact |

The report was produced from this same export. That is what makes the next table meaningful.

## 2. What didn't

The report labels a column **"Voluntary Attrition."** It reproduces our **total** attrition — including involuntary exits — to one decimal place, in all seven departments:

| Department | AR "voluntary attrition" | Our **total** attrition | Our **voluntary-only** | Annualised voluntary on active |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retail Banking | 9.3% | **9.3%** ✅ | 7.7% | 4.2% |
| Technology | 10.4% | **10.4%** ✅ | 8.5% | 4.7% |
| Risk & Compliance | 11.8% | **11.8%** ✅ | 9.6% | 5.5% |
| Insurance | 10.3% | **10.3%** ✅ | 8.0% | 4.5% |
| Wealth Management | 10.5% | **10.5%** ✅ | 7.9% | 4.4% |
| Corporate Operations | 11.6% | **11.6%** ✅ | 9.7% | 5.5% |
| Executive Leadership | 8.3% | **8.3%** ✅ | 7.4% | 4.0% |
| **Group** | **10.4%** | **10.4%** ✅ | **8.5%** | **4.7%** |

Seven independent exact matches is not coincidence. The published metric is total attrition — it includes the **267 involuntary** exits (redundancy, performance and conduct).

## 3. The second issue: the period

The report describes 1,400 departures as occurring "during the financial year." In the dataset, those 1,400 exits span the **full two-year** observation window:

| Period | Voluntary | Involuntary | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2024 | 214 | 53 | 267 |
| FY2025 | 683 | 163 | **846** |
| FY2026 (H1) | 236 | 51 | 287 |
| **Total** | **1,133** | **267** | **1,400** |

So 10.4% is a **two-year cumulative rate on the total roster**, presented as an annual rate. Like-for-like — voluntary only, annualised, on active headcount — the figure is **4.7% per year**.

## 4. Why this matters commercially

Pillar 4 of the Annual Report commits to *"resolve voluntary attrition to below 9.5% by FY2026,"* and the FY2026 guidance table repeats it. That target is tracked against a number that is neither voluntary nor annual.

- On the **published** basis, NovaCorp reads 10.4% and is **missing** the target.
- On a **strict voluntary** reading, it is at 8.5% cumulative — or **4.7% annualised** — and has **already met** it.

The risk is that remediation budget gets aimed at a headline the company has arguably already hit, while the concentrated, genuinely expensive problem — high-value voluntary loss inside Entity_B — sits outside how the metric is framed and therefore outside what gets funded.

## 5. How we handled it

We **restate; we do not accuse.** All rates in this deck are annualised voluntary-on-active, footnoted on every slide that carries one.

**Caveat.** We infer the definition from an exact numerical match across seven departments. That is strong evidence, but it is not a substitute for confirming the intended definition with NovaCorp's HR reporting team. It is equally possible the labelling error sits in the underlying export rather than the report. Either way the two do not currently agree, and the discrepancy should be resolved before the FY2026 target is tracked against it.
